Apply for a consultant listing

For working AI consultants who are actively taking on clients. Identity verified, paid placement, disclosed on the card.

The thirteen profiles on this site are editorial — those people did not ask to be there and do not pay to be there. This page is a different thing entirely: a small number of paid listings for consultants who actually want the enquiries.

What we verify — and what we do not

Verification here means identity verification only. Before a listing goes live we confirm, and keep a dated record of:

  • Your business's registration number, checked against the public register in your jurisdiction
  • Any professional licence or accreditation you claim, checked against the issuing body
  • A working business address, website and direct contact route
  • Your written confirmation that you want to be listed, and on what terms
  • The date each check was carried out

This is never an endorsement of the quality of your work. We do not audit your projects, call your clients, or form a view on whether you are any good. Your card says exactly that, in those words. If you want a directory that will imply we vouch for your competence, this is the wrong one.

What a listing card shows

A fixed format, the same for everyone, with no ranking and no way to buy a better position:

  • Your business name, as registered
  • What you do, in your own words, up to 40 words — factual claims only
  • Where you are based and which markets you serve
  • Your website and one contact route
  • The line “Listed consultant — paid placement, identity verified on [date]”, always, on the card itself

There is no rating, no star, no review score, no badge, no "top rated" tier and no featured slot, because we hold no review data and will not manufacture any. Listings are ordered alphabetically.

What it costs

We are not publishing a price yet. Listings open when the first verified consultant is approved, and we would rather quote you honestly than post a number we invented before anyone had asked. Write to us and we will tell you the rate, the term and the refund position in the same email.

How to apply

Email shane@warrenbg.com with the subject line Consultant listing, and include:

  1. Your registered business name and registration number
  2. The jurisdiction it is registered in
  3. Your website and the contact route you want listed
  4. The 40 words you want on the card
  5. Any professional licence or accreditation you want checked

We reply to every application, including the ones we decline. We decline where the business cannot be found on a public register, where the copy makes claims we cannot check, or where the work described is something we would not want to send a reader toward. There is no charge for applying and no charge if we decline.

How these profiles are compiled. Every profile on this site is written from publicly available material — the subject's own site, published interviews, press coverage and public posts — and every factual claim links to where we got it. We do not verify, rate, rank or endorse anyone profiled here. Nobody profiled has been interviewed, reference-checked or asked for permission, and nobody profiled is affiliated with, represented by, or paying this site. Where a figure comes from a person's own marketing rather than an independent source, we say so on the page.

Opinions are labelled as opinions. Where public criticism of someone exists we report that it exists and link to it; we do not adopt it. This is editorial commentary, not a rating, and it is no substitute for your own due diligence before hiring anyone.
