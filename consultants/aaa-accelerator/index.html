AAA Accelerator

Liam Ottley's paid AI Automation Agency mentorship programme

What they are known for

A note on what this entry is. AAA Accelerator is not a separate business from Liam Ottley — it is his paid programme. We list it separately because people search for it by name and arrive expecting information about the programme rather than the person, but the two entries describe one operation and you should read them together.

Notable public work

It is a paid mentorship programme teaching people to build and run an AI automation agency: how to package chatbots, voice agents and workflow automation, and how to sell them to small and medium businesses. Third-party reviews consistently describe it as roughly a six-month programme and report pricing in the region of $5,000 to $7,150 — the price is not published on the site and is disclosed on a sales call, so treat the reported figure as reported, not confirmed. Public reviews are collected on Trustpilot.

What is being said publicly

A correction to our own earlier entry. We previously called this "the leading accelerator for AI agency owners" with "hundreds of graduates". We had no source for either and have removed both. The substantive criticisms in circulation are that the AAA market is saturated; that the programme sells education about a business model more effectively than the model itself performs; and that some buyers report high-pressure sales, including difficulty obtaining contract or refund terms before payment. Several of the loudest critics sell competing programmes. If you are considering it, the single most useful thing you can do is insist on seeing the contract and refund terms in writing before any payment — a reasonable request that a legitimate programme will meet.
