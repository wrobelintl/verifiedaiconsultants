Alex Finn

Founder & CEO, Henry Intelligent Machines and Creator Buddy · OpenClaw power user

OpenClawAgentsVibe Coding

What they are known for

Alex Finn is the most visible example of a specific 2026 phenomenon: the one-person company run substantially by agents. He is founder and CEO of Henry Intelligent Machines PBC and of Creator Buddy, an AI writing tool for creators on X (LinkedIn).

Notable public work

He became one of the most-cited OpenClaw power users after documenting a multi-agent setup running on local hardware — by his own posting, three Mac Studios, an Nvidia DGX Spark and a Mac Mini clustered with EXO. An interview with ChatPRD walks through the resulting workflow. His YouTube channel teaches Claude Code, Codex and agent tooling to people who have never used them.

What is being said publicly

Two cautions worth stating plainly. First, his revenue figures — Creator Buddy at $300,000 ARR, $100,000 of sales in the first fifteen minutes after launch — are self-reported and have not been independently verified. Second, his best-known setups are demonstrations of what is possible with significant hardware and a very high tolerance for breakage, not a template a small business should copy directly. Read him for the frontier of what agents can do; do not read him as an implementation plan.
