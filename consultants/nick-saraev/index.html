Nick Saraev

Founder, Maker School · Make.com and n8n automation educator

AutomationNo-codeMake.com

What they are known for

Nick Saraev teaches people to build and sell automations, working primarily in Make.com and n8n. His distinguishing feature against most of this category is that his public material is mostly build-alongs — here is the scenario, here are the modules, here is what broke — rather than motivational framing.

Notable public work

He runs Maker School, a paid programme aimed at getting members to a first paying automation client, which advertises a first-client-in-90-days or money-back guarantee, 218 videos, end-to-end Make and n8n courses and 40+ copy-paste templates. He publishes free workflow walkthroughs on YouTube and at nicksaraev.com.

What is being said publicly

The numbers attached to his name in public — roughly $300,000 in three months at 94% margins, automations sold between $1,500 and $10,000, $20,000 a month sourced through an Upwork filtering workflow — all originate from his own sales and marketing material. They are self-reported and we have seen no independent verification of any of them. That is not an accusation; it is the correct label, and it applies to almost every income figure in this corner of the market. The free workflow content stands on its own and can be judged directly, which is the sounder basis for a decision.
